Locator FAQ

Everything you need to find us today.

How do I know where the truck is today?
Check the location cards above — today's live stop is also pinned on the map. We post the daily pin on Instagram every morning by 7:30am.
Do the locations change on weekends?
Yes. Friday to Sunday we move to event venues and food parks. Weekday mornings are always the Ammapet truck stop.
Can I book a private location?
Absolutely — private catering is our specialty. Head to the Contact page or call +91 427 234 5679 and we'll bring the truck to you.
Do you accept card and UPI payments at the truck?
We accept cash, all major cards, UPI and every popular wallet. Online orders through the Shop page are prepaid.

About Stackly

Stackly food truck rolled on to the streets of Salem in March, 2015. And with it, they took the city by storm with everyone's favorite comfort food: grilled cheese sandwiches, tacos — only not quite as innocent as mom used to make!
