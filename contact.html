Frequently Asked

Quick answers before you write to us.

How far in advance should I book catering?
Two weeks is ideal for events over 50 guests. Smaller gatherings can often be arranged within 3–4 days — just call and ask.
Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?
Always. The full veggie menu runs alongside the meat menu at the truck, and catering packages can be made fully vegan on request.
Which areas in Salem do you deliver to?
Fairlands, Hasthampatti, Junction, Ammapet, Yercaud Road and Suramangalam — plus anywhere within 15km of the fort for orders over ₹499.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes — call us within 10 minutes of ordering and we'll fix it. Once the griddle is on, the order is locked in.

About Stackly

Stackly food truck rolled on to the streets of Salem in March, 2015. And with it, they took the city by storm with everyone's favorite comfort food: grilled cheese sandwiches, tacos — only not quite as innocent as mom used to make!
