Serving Salem Since 2015

Stackly started as one small truck parked outside Fort Road, and we have been rolling ever since. Today you'll find us at food parks, office districts and street corners across the city — grilling, folding and serving tacos the way they should be: fresh, fast and full of flavour.

Authentic Tacos

Every tortilla is warmed to order, every salsa made from scratch each morning. From smokey pulled pork to our vegan rucola garbanzo, the Stackly menu is built on family recipes, local produce and a whole lot of lime. Gluten-free and vegan options always available.

About Stackly

Stackly food truck rolled on to the streets of Salem in March, 2015. And with it, they took the city by storm with everyone's favorite comfort food: grilled cheese sandwiches, tacos — only not quite as innocent as mom used to make!
