How We Cook It

Three steps between the farm and your hands.

1

Source Fresh

Produce, meat and tortillas are bought fresh from Salem markets every single morning — nothing sits overnight.

2

Grill To Order

Nothing is pre-made. Your taco hits the griddle after you order, so it reaches you hot off the flat-top.

3

Serve Fast

Our crew plates, packs and hands over most orders in under four minutes — even during the lunch rush.

About Stackly

Stackly food truck rolled on to the streets of Salem in March, 2015. And with it, they took the city by storm with everyone's favorite comfort food: grilled cheese sandwiches, tacos — only not quite as innocent as mom used to make!
